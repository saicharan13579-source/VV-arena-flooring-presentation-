The brief

What each floor has to handle

Every zone of the venue is judged on four demands. The right floor meets them at the lowest lifetime cost, not just the lowest build cost.

Load

Forklifts, stage trucks, racking and heavy point loads from event rigging.

Chemicals

Spills, cleaning agents and food-safety audits in kitchens and wash areas.

Heat & wet

Hot wash-downs, steam and cold rooms that shock ordinary coatings.

Flatness

Tall storage racks and wheeled equipment need a level, true floor.

How each is built on site

Resin and finish systems go on after the slab has cured, so they add time at the end of the programme. Trimix, laser screed and hardener are built with the slab itself.

Quality on site

What every good floor needs

Most failures, such as cracking, curling or peeling coatings, start in the base slab. These checks go into the specification and are inspected on site.

Tested subgrade

Compacted and load-tested before the slab.

Right concrete grade

Typically M25–M30 for industrial slabs.

Vapour barrier

Under the slab, so coatings don’t blister.

Joints cut on time

Usually within about 24 hours of the pour.

Proper curing

Water-cure for 14 days or more.

Moisture tested

Below 4–5% before any resin coating.
